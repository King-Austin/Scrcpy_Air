# Wireless Screen Mirroring with Scrcpy

Your phone (**TECNO KI7**, IP: `10.19.56.62`) is now configured for wireless screen mirroring over your Wi-Fi network.

---

## 🚀 How to Launch Wireless Mirroring Anytime

### Option 1: Quick Connect (Double-Click)
Simply double-click:
👉 **`scrcpy-wireless-connect.bat`**
- Press **[Enter]** to connect to your saved phone IP (`10.19.56.62`), or type a new IP if your router assigns one.
- The wireless mirror window will open immediately!
- **No USB cable needed!**

---

### Option 2: After Phone Reboot (One-time Re-initializer)
If your phone is ever powered off or rebooted, Android turns off port 5555 listening by default:
1. Connect your phone to your PC with the USB cable.
2. Double-click:
   👉 **`scrcpy-wireless-setup.bat`**
3. The script will automatically enable wireless mode, save your IP, and launch the mirror.
4. **Unplug the USB cable** once it says SUCCESS!

---

## ⌨️ Useful Scrcpy Shortcuts
- **Fullscreen**: `Alt` + `F`
- **Turn phone screen off while mirroring**: `Alt` + `O`
- **Turn phone screen back on**: `Alt` + `Shift` + `O`
- **Copy PC clipboard to phone**: `Ctrl` + `C` on PC then `Ctrl` + `V` into phone
- **Volume Up / Down**: `Alt` + `Up` / `Alt` + `Down`
- **Home**: `Alt` + `H`
- **Back**: `Alt` + `B` or Right-Click
- **Switch Apps / Recents**: `Alt` + `S`
